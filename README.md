# 🏫 HALL ALERT

**Making hostel maintenance easier, one report at a time.**

Are you a student at **Babcock University**? Tired of having to remind the hall porters every day about that leaking tap, faulty light, or other maintenance issue in your room?

**Hall Alert is here to sort that out!**

Hall Alert is a platform that makes reporting hostel maintenance issues easier and more convenient. With just a tap of a button, you, yes you can submit complaints directly to the hall management, track the progress of your reports, and get an estimated time when the issue will be resolved, all from the comfort of your room.

No more repeated reminders. No more wondering if your complaint has been heard. Just a simpler, automated way to get things fixed.
